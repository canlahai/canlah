# CanLah Programme Planner — quick start for the main contractor

A shared schedule your whole team keeps up to date. You set it up, invite your
subcontractors, and everyone updates their own work. No more chasing status over
WhatsApp.

You'll get a login from the CanLah team to start.

---

## 1. Log in
Go to the link CanLah gives you and sign in with your email and password.

## 2. Create your programme
Open **Programme Planner** → enter your project name and start date → **Create**.

## 3. Add your activities
Add the work items (e.g. "Cast slab L3", "Bore pile P12"). For each, set:
- how many working days it takes,
- what it depends on (which activity must finish first).

The Gantt chart and critical path update as you type. (Tell CanLah if you'd like help
loading these from your BQ / drawings instead of typing them.)

## 4. Add your team
Open **Team & access** on the programme. For each person, enter their **email** and pick:

- **What they can do:**
  - **Admin** — run the team, invite people, edit everything (you, your PM).
  - **Editor** — update activities, tick checklists, log deliveries (engineers, subs doing the work).
  - **Viewer** — look only, can't change anything (client, consultant).
- **Their trade** — PM, Engineer, Procurement, Subcon, or Supervisor (just a label).

Click **Create invite**, copy the link, and send it to them (WhatsApp / email).

> Note: each person needs a CanLah account under that email first. Ask CanLah to set up
> accounts for your team, then send the invite links.

When they open the link and sign in, they tap **Accept & join** and they're on the team.

## 5. Everyone keeps their part updated
Each person opens an activity (the **Plan** button on its row) and updates:
- **Status** — to do / in progress / done / **blocked** (with a reason + who's responsible).
- **Checklist** — pre-task inspections (e.g. before casting: formwork, rebar, survey).
  Tap each: Pending → Comply → Doesn't comply. Use **Suggest** to auto-load the
  standard list for that task.
- **Materials / deliveries** — what's needed, by when, who's responsible. A late delivery
  flags the activity automatically.

## 6. Check it daily (you, the main con)
- **Activity log** — everything the team changed, newest first. One place to see status
  instead of calling around.
- **At-risk** count + filter — what's blocked or not ready to start.

---

## What "good" looks like in week 1
- Your subs log in on their own and update their work.
- A delay shows up with a name on it ("concrete delayed — Procurement"), not a mystery.
- You check the activity log instead of WhatsApping for updates.

If that's happening, it's working. If only you are touching it after a few days, tell
CanLah — that's useful signal, and we'll adjust.
