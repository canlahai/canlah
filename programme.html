📄 Build from your scope of works

Upload your contract / scope-of-works document (PDF or image). We read every work item, its type and duration, and draft a programme you can edit and share. Durations come from your document.

📊 Build from a Bill of Quantities

Upload your BQ (PDF or image). We read the measured quantities, estimate durations from productivity rates, and draft a sequenced programme. We also read the project profile to pre-fill the generator.

⚠ Durations are productivity-rate estimates — a planner must verify before any submission.

📥 Import a plan

Bring an existing schedule into CanLah. We support MS Project XML (.xml), Primavera P6 (.xer), CSV, and PDF / image (AI reads it). MS Project's .mpp is proprietary — in MS Project use File ▸ Save As ▸ XML and upload that.

⚠ Imported dates/durations are read as-is; the critical path is recomputed on the SG working calendar — verify before any submission.

✨ Generate a draft programme

Answer a few questions and we draft a standard Singapore construction Master Programme — full WBS, floor-cycle super-structure, BCA / SCDF / TOP / CSC gates, and a computed critical path. Edit anything after; durations are benchmark placeholders.
